SPORTING CP ASCENT

At age 12, Ronaldo attended a three-day trial with Sporting CP, one of the biggest clubs in Portugal. Sporting signed him for a fee of £1,500, requiring him to move from his home in Madeira to the mainland in Lisbon. Training and Development: He attended Sporting’s famous youth academy, the Academia de Alcochete. Rapid Progression: He became the first player in the club’s history to play for the under-16, under-17, under-18, B-team, and first team all within a single season. Professional Debut: At age 16, he was promoted to the first team by manager László Bölöni. He made his official debut on August 14, 2002, in a UEFA Champions League qualifying match against Inter Milan. He scored his first professional goals against Moreirense in October 2002.
