THE ROOTS OF A GOAT

Born in the poverty-stricken neighborhood of Santo António in Funchal, Madeira, Cristiano's playground was the concrete patches and steep hills of a tiny island. Sharing a single room with three siblings, he had nothing but old patched-up sneakers and a cheap plastic ball. It was here, amidst the struggle and heavy rain, that the unstoppable drive and raw obsession were forged.
