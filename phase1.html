THE ROOTS OF A GOAT

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born in the poverty-stricken neighborhood of Santo António in Funchal, Madeira. His football journey began on concrete streets and steep hills. Andorinha (1992–1995): Joined at age seven, coached indirectly by his father who worked as kit man. Nacional (1995–1997): Rapid growth, captaincy, and early recognition.
