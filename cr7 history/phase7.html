The One-Man Army:
• Carrying the Nation: Often faced with limited squad depth, Ronaldo acted as a one-man army, single-handedly dragging Portugal through qualifiers and tournament stages through sheer force of will.

Iconic Hat-tricks:
• Sweden (2013): A heroic hat-trick in the World Cup play-offs secured Portugal's spot in the tournament.
• Spain (2018): An incredible hat-trick, capped by a late free-kick, forced a 3-3 draw in the World Cup group stage.

Unprecedented Longevity:
He became the only player to score in six different World Cups. His 2026 World Cup campaign featured his first-ever knockout stage goal, helping Portugal defeat Croatia in the Round of 32. Through his tireless discipline, he transformed Portugal from underdogs into a consistent global powerhouse.
